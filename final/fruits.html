Here is my Top 10 list for Fruits.
  1. Mango It’s very sweet and tangy at the same time.
  2. Strawberry It has a rich flavor that I like and I like strawberry shakes.
  3. Kiwi It’s just a different fruit to me and I like that.
  4. Raspberry I’ve always liked these and they are easy to eat. I’m just not a fan of the seeds in smoothies.
  5. Green Grapes They can be sour at times and I like the crunch.
  6. Purple Grapes I like the flavor and crunch as well.
  7. Peach It gives me the vibes of an apple but I like the taste of peach better.
  8. Banana The taste is good but I don’t like the stringy part of it.
  9. Dragon Fruit I like how this fruit is different but it is tasteless so not the most enjoyable to eat.
  10. Blueberry These are good but they are always hit and miss with me.
